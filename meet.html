Stewardship, in plain English

Someone should be responsible for connecting the whole.

A major residential project can involve architects, designers, engineers, contractors, trades, vendors, inspectors, permitting authorities, and hundreds of homeowner decisions. Each participant may be responsible for one part. Crewzz organizes the agreed responsibilities around the homeowner's interests.

Step 01

Start by understanding the homeowner, not selling a scope.

The project, property, priorities, timing, concerns, and current conditions are reviewed to determine whether Crewzz is the right fit and what should happen next.

Concept imagery

The sequence matters. Design decisions affect engineering. Engineering affects permits. Specifications affect procurement. Procurement affects schedule. Construction decisions affect how the home can be maintained later.

Crewzz does not replace every licensed professional involved. It provides the Stewardship structure that helps connect their agreed work around the homeowner.

A commercial model you can understand

The fee should not grow because your project costs more.

Crewzz charges a disclosed flat monthly fee. We do not add a percentage markup to approved pass-through project costs for materials, trade labor, or change orders. You approve the spending. Crewzz provides the documentation, review, coordination, and visibility needed to make that decision with context.

01

Flat monthly fee

Crewzz's disclosed monthly fee does not rise because you choose a more expensive finish or approve additional trade work.

02

No percentage markup

Approved pass-through costs for materials, trade labor, and change orders are not increased by a Crewzz percentage markup.

03

Completion-date accountability

If Crewzz misses the qualifying completion date, our monthly fee stops.

The On-Time Completion Fee Guarantee is subject to the agreed project schedule, documented date adjustments, qualifying conditions, and the terms of the applicable client agreement. It applies to Crewzz's monthly fee and does not guarantee that physical completion will occur by a particular date regardless of circumstances. Any separate service charge or vendor compensation is disclosed before approval.

Straight answers

What homeowners should know about Crewzz.

What is Crewzz?

Crewzz is a residential Stewardship company. We coordinate design, construction, documentation, verification, and ongoing care through one homeowner-centered framework. Exact responsibilities vary by service and project agreement.

How is Crewzz compensated?

Crewzz charges a disclosed flat monthly fee. We do not add a percentage markup to approved pass-through project costs for materials, trade labor, or change orders. Any separate service charge or vendor compensation is disclosed before approval.

What is the On-Time Completion Fee Guarantee?

If Crewzz misses the qualifying completion date, our monthly fee stops. The guarantee is subject to the agreed project schedule, documented date adjustments, qualifying conditions, and the applicable client agreement.

Does Crewzz perform every licensed service?

No. Appropriate licensed architects, engineers, contractors, consultants, inspectors, and specialty providers remain responsible for work within their respective disciplines. Crewzz coordinates the responsibilities included in the applicable project agreement.

How is SOD Home Group related to Crewzz?

Oren Dagan founded SOD Home Group in 2008. That construction and remodeling experience informed Crewzz, but SOD Home Group's project history is not presented as Crewzz's project record.
