Our process

Design Different

Crewzz.Design is a coordinated residential design and planning process that helps homeowners resolve the vision, feasibility, specifications, systems, and critical decisions before construction.

Step 01

Begin with the life the home must support.

We start with your priorities, routines, property, concerns, and vision. This establishes the design intent and the decisions the project must answer.

Concept imagery

The order matters. Direction is explored while it is still flexible. Materials and systems are coordinated before they reach the field. The design is then reviewed and visualized in greater detail.

Construction always contains variables. The goal is to identify more of them earlier, while they can still be addressed as design decisions instead of field problems.

Design that performs

It has to work beyond the rendering.

A design can look compelling and still leave important conditions unresolved. Architecture, interiors, engineering, materials, equipment, and approvals may each be developed by different participants.

Crewzz brings those decisions into one coordinated structure around the homeowner’s interests. The objective is a design the project team can understand, evaluate, and carry forward with fewer unresolved conditions.

Not for every project

Built for homeowners who want to resolve more before they build.

Thoughtful planning takes time and participation. You will be asked to make important decisions earlier, consider tradeoffs, and engage with details that are easier to change in design than during construction.

Crewzz accepts Design engagements by application, project fit, and current capacity.
