Accessibility

A website should be usable.

Crewzz is working toward a clear, inclusive digital experience for people with disabilities.

On this page

Our commitmentCurrent featuresGet help

Our goal is to design and maintain public digital experiences with WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a reference standard, while continuing to identify and address barriers.

This is a commitment to ongoing work, not a claim of perfect conformance. Third-party forms, videos, community platforms, and other embedded tools may have accessibility behavior outside Crewzz's direct control.

Accessibility features

  • Semantic headings and landmarks on new pages
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus behavior
  • Skip links and descriptive page titles
  • Text alternatives for meaningful imagery
  • Responsive layouts and reduced-motion support
  • Color and typography choices intended to support readability

Need assistance?

If you encounter a barrier, need information in another format, or need help completing a digital process, email info@crewzz.com or call +1 844 309 0999.

Please identify the page, the feature, the assistive technology or browser you use if relevant, and the best way to reach you. We will make a reasonable effort to provide access or an alternative method.

Feedback and updates

Accessibility is part of product maintenance. We review feedback, test representative journeys, and update this statement as the site and its integrations change.
