The framework

Why it existsSeven stagesStraight answers

A major home project can go wrong even when every individual is capable. The trouble often starts between the people, contracts, decisions, and responsibilities.

Nobody should expect the homeowner to coordinate the whole system alone.

Architects, designers, engineers, contractors, trades, vendors, authorities, and inspectors each own a part. Crewzz keeps those parts connected around the homeowner's complete outcome. We call that Stewardship.

Crewzz does not replace licensed professionals. Appropriate providers remain responsible for their disciplines. Crewzz's role is defined by each service and project agreement.

The Crewzz path

  1. Project fit

    We review the property, goals, timing, concerns, location, scope, professional requirements, and current capacity before recommending a path.

  2. Project definition

    The homeowner's priorities, decision-makers, responsibilities, constraints, and definition of success are placed on the record.

  3. Coordinated design

    Architecture, interiors, engineering, systems, selections, budget assumptions, and unresolved decisions are connected for the agreed scope.

  4. Build readiness

    Construction commitment waits until the agreed drawings, scope, critical selections, responsibilities, schedule assumptions, procurement plan, and major risks are sufficiently defined.

  5. Construction coordination

    Field leadership, trades, information, schedule, changes, approvals, documentation, and payment support follow the agreed operating structure.

  6. Verification and handover

    Consequential work and records move through defined review points, qualified specialist input, and documented closeout requirements.

  7. Ongoing care

    Where LIVE is available, the home's record, planned maintenance, service coordination, and issue triage continue through a property-specific relationship.

Straight answers

What does Crewzz do?

Crewzz coordinates residential design, construction, documentation, verification, and ongoing care through one homeowner-centered framework. Exact responsibilities vary by service and agreement.

Does Crewzz perform every licensed service?

No. Qualified licensed professionals remain responsible for work within their disciplines. Crewzz coordinates the responsibilities included in the applicable agreement.

Can any project hire Crewzz?

No. Engagements are accepted by application and reviewed for fit, scope, timing, geography, licensing requirements, specialist availability, and capacity.
