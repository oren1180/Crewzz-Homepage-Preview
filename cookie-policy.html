This policy explains how Crewzz and service providers may use cookies, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies on Crewzz digital services.

Production integration required. The final consent tool must be configured after the approved analytics, advertising, CRM, video, and embedded-form vendors are confirmed. Nonessential tags should respect the choices required by applicable law.

What these technologies do

Cookies can remember preferences, keep a service secure, help forms work, measure traffic, identify errors, and evaluate communications or campaigns.

Types we may use

Essential

Required for security, navigation, form operation, consent records, and other core functions.

Analytics and performance

Help us understand visits, engagement, site reliability, and how people find or use our content.

Advertising and communications

May help measure campaigns, limit repetition, connect a response to a source, or tailor follow-up where permitted.

Preferences and embedded media

Remember settings or allow third-party video, scheduling, community, or form features to operate.

Your choices

You can manage cookies in your browser, delete stored data, use privacy extensions, or decline nonessential technologies through the production consent interface where available. Blocking some technologies may affect site features.

You may also contact info@crewzz.com with a privacy question. See the Privacy Policy for additional rights and request instructions.
