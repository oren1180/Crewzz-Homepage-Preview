How it works

From measurement to a written record.

A wellness room is easy to add. A residence whose environmental decisions are measured, specified, coordinated, and documented is harder. The process begins with information, not products.

Step 01

Begin by making the invisible visible.

MAVI’s Snapshot uses available address-level environmental information to provide an initial view of conditions around the residence. It is an entry point, not a medical assessment or a substitute for project-specific testing.

The specification layer

A home should support the life you live in it.

The air at the pillow. The water at every fixture. The light after sunset. The sound that follows you into the bedroom. The materials surrounding your family. These conditions are easy to overlook because they are part of the background.

MAVI organizes them across ten domains and 129 factors. Crewzz coordinates applicable recommendations with the people responsible for the architecture, engineering, interiors, materials, systems, controls, procurement, and verification. Research informs the specification. No medical outcome is promised.

MAVI was founded by Kas Bordier. Its published framework draws on more than 200 cited studies, and its longevity architecture work with Kelly Hoppen CBE was featured by Financial Times House & Home in May 2026.

Straight answers

Before you apply.

What is longevity architecture?

Longevity architecture applies research-informed environmental considerations to the design and operation of a home. It can include air, water, light, sound, temperature, materials, technology, and the daily patterns the residence supports.

Who is MAVI?

MAVI is a longevity architecture consultancy founded by Kas Bordier. Its published framework organizes residential environmental considerations across ten domains and 129 factors, with recommendations connected to cited research.

Who can apply?

Available to Crewzz Estate clients only. Projects are reviewed for fit, geography, timing, specialist availability, and current capacity.

Is this medical care?

No. This is residential planning, coordination, and educational information. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a guarantee of sleep, recovery, longevity, or any other health outcome.

Can this be introduced into an existing residence?

Potentially. Feasibility depends on the home, existing systems, project scope, specialist input, budget, and the extent of renovation being considered.
