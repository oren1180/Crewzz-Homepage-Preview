Crewzz Secure × ASERO

A safer home is designed that way from the beginning.

Security should not begin with an alarm package. Crewzz Secure coordinates the physical, digital, human, and operational layers of residential protection while the architecture can still respond. Without one protection concept, the home inherits a collection of devices instead of coordinated security infrastructure.

How it works

Design the protection before selecting the equipment.

Most residential security begins with a catalogue. Crewzz Secure begins with the residence, the household, the relevant exposure, and the decisions that will become difficult or expensive to change after construction.

Step 01

Start with what must remain private.

The residence, household priorities, project stage, and appropriate information boundaries are established before sensitive details are shared or solutions are discussed.

Concept imagery

Six of the seven stages happen before final handover because the critical advantage is not a particular device. It is the ability to coordinate protection while architecture, engineering, technology, and access decisions are still open.

Exact scope, methods, specialists, regulated services, testing, and responsibilities are defined privately for each accepted project. The public page explains the discipline without publishing a protection playbook.

Concept imagery

The reason why

After the walls close, options disappear.

Security touches architecture before it touches equipment. Openings, circulation, visibility, infrastructure pathways, power, communications, technology rooms, exterior planning, and operating procedures influence what can be coordinated later.

When protection enters after design, the project is forced to work around finished decisions. That often means more compromise, more visible correction, and fewer choices. Early planning creates room for security, privacy, function, and design to be resolved together.

Crewzz Secure is informed by ASERO’s published threat-based and security-by-design methodology. ASERO describes an integrated approach across physical, cyber, and human factors, with protection considered from the earliest planning stages. Review ASERO’s methodology.

Layered residential protection

One home. Four connected layers.

No single device protects a residence. The protection concept must consider the property, its systems, the people with access, and how the household continues to function when something changes. The specific measures remain private. The planning disciplines do not.

  1. 01

    Physical

    The site, building envelope, circulation, access, visibility, and infrastructure required for the approved concept.

  2. 02

    Digital

    Residential networks, connected devices, data pathways, administration, updates, and appropriate separation.

  3. 03

    Human

    Household routines, staff, vendors, credentials, permissions, information handling, and changes in access.

  4. 04

    Continuity

    How essential residential functions, communications, documentation, and responsibilities continue during disruption.

Private by design

Serious protection requires discretion.

Available to Crewzz Estate clients only. Crewzz Secure reaches across residential architecture, engineering, technology, privacy, household operations, specialist work, and controlled information. It cannot be treated as a generic add-on.

Engagements are reviewed by application and accepted based on residential project fit, timing, geography, specialist availability, appropriate scope, and current capacity. Crewzz does not promise that any residence or system can be made invulnerable.

Straight answers

Before you apply.

What is residential security by design?

It means considering relevant physical, digital, operational, and human factors during planning so approved infrastructure can be coordinated with the architecture before construction limits the available options.

Who is ASERO?

ASERO is a security and resilience consultancy founded and chaired by Doron Bergerbest-Eilon, a former Director of Protection and Security for the State of Israel. ASERO’s published methodology emphasizes threat-based assessment, layered protection, resilience, and security by design.

Is invisible security always better?

No. Visible and concealed measures serve different purposes. The appropriate balance is a project-specific security decision, not an aesthetic slogan. Crewzz coordinates the approved approach with the residential design.

Does Crewzz provide monitoring or armed protection?

Not through this webpage. Crewzz Secure is presented here as residential planning and coordination. Any regulated installation, monitoring, cybersecurity, protective, architectural, engineering, or specialty services are performed by appropriately qualified providers under the applicable project agreements.

Can this be introduced into an existing residence?

Potentially. Feasibility depends on the property, existing construction, systems, renovation scope, applicable specialists, and how much finished work can reasonably be opened or modified.

Who can apply?

Available to Crewzz Estate clients only. Engagements are reviewed for residential project fit, timing, geography, specialist availability, appropriate scope, and current capacity.
