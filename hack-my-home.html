The rules are part of the product

A community, not a lead list.

Trust disappears the moment a homeowner’s question becomes someone else’s private sales opportunity. These standards protect the room.

Members are Home Hackers

No unsolicited contractor chasing

Professionals may contribute knowledge. Private sales approaches a member did not request are prohibited and may result in removal.

Disclose your role

Contractors, designers, vendors, and other professionals identify who they are and any relevant interest.

Show the useful details

Specific questions, photos, drawings, and numbers create more useful discussion. Protect private information before posting.

Help the next homeowner

When the room helps you decide, come back and share what happened. The next Home Hacker may be facing the same choice.

Straight answers

Before you request access.

Is Hack My Home free?

Joining Hack My Home through this invitation is free. Joining does not obligate you to hire Crewzz or purchase a paid service.

What happens after I request an invitation?

Complete the Crewzz form, receive the access instructions using the contact information you provide, and follow the final access step for the community hosted on GoKollab.

Will professionals contact me to sell services?

Unsolicited contractor chasing and private sales approaches are prohibited. If it happens, report it to the community moderators.

Is this project-specific professional advice?

No. Community education and discussion do not replace legal, architectural, engineering, financial, insurance, medical, safety, or licensed trade advice for your specific project.
