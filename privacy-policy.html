This policy explains how Crewzz Inc. collects and handles personal information through crewzz.com, project inquiries, community registrations, communications, and related digital services.

Production review required. This policy is a substantive website draft. Counsel must confirm the final data map, vendors, retention practices, state notices, and consent flows before launch.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identifiers and contact information, including name, email, telephone number, mailing address, and account details.
  • Project and property information you submit, including location, scope, budget range, timing, interests, plans, images, and communications.
  • Community, event, newsletter, and support preferences.
  • Device and usage information, including IP address, browser, pages viewed, referring source, and cookie or similar technology data.
  • Records of consent, requests, approvals, and communications.

How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and assess project fit.
  • Provide requested services, content, community access, support, and communications.
  • Operate, secure, analyze, and improve our websites and workflows.
  • Measure marketing performance and personalize communications where permitted.
  • Comply with law, enforce agreements, prevent misuse, and protect rights and safety.

How we disclose information

We may disclose information to hosting, analytics, CRM, communications, scheduling, payment, security, professional, and other service providers that support our operations. We may also disclose information to project professionals or partners when requested, authorized, or necessary for a service; in a business transaction; or when required by law.

We do not disclose personal information merely because another contractor wants to solicit Hack My Home members. Community rules and the applicable platform terms govern member conduct.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, contractual and operational needs, dispute resolution, legal obligations, and recordkeeping. We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of certain personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. We will verify requests as required and will not unlawfully discriminate for exercising applicable rights.

You may adjust browser settings, use available cookie controls, unsubscribe through an email link, or contact us. Where legally recognized and technically detectable, we will evaluate browser-based opt-out preference signals under applicable law.

Children and international visitors

Our services are not directed to children under 13. If you access the site outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other jurisdictions whose laws differ from yours.

Updates

We may update this policy as our services, vendors, or legal obligations change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
