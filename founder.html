The missing responsibility

A project can have a full team and still have no one responsible for the whole.

Every specialist may perform their part well. Yet scope, sequence, decisions, costs, documentation, and handoffs can still live in separate places. When nobody owns the connections, the homeowner inherits them.

Crewzz calls the answer Stewardship: a homeowner-centered framework around the agreed people, information, responsibilities, and decisions.

Reason before promise

Alignment has to be designed into the model.

In percentage-based arrangements, compensation may rise when approved project cost rises. That does not describe every professional or contract. It is a structural risk homeowners deserve to understand before they commit.

Crewzz uses a different compensation structure so its fee is not calculated as a percentage of approved pass-through project costs.

02No percentage markup on approved pass-through costs

Crewzz does not add a percentage markup to approved pass-through project costs for materials, trade labor, or change orders.

03A financial consequence for missing the qualifying date

Under the On-Time Completion Fee Guarantee, Crewzz stops charging its monthly fee if it misses the qualifying completion date.

Separate service charges and any vendor compensation are disclosed before approval. The applicable agreement controls scope, fees, dates, documented adjustments, qualifying conditions, and remedies.

Experience without borrowed proof

SOD taught me the work. Crewzz carries the lesson forward.

SOD Home Group is the separate design-build firm I founded in 2008. Its history is evidence of my construction and remodeling experience. It is not Crewzz's project record.

Crewzz must earn trust through its own agreements, people, systems, decisions, and results. Founder experience can inform the standard. It cannot substitute for company proof.

The principle is simple: respect the craft, make the structure visible, and never ask a homeowner to confuse a founder's history with a company's record.

Questions, answered

About Oren and Crewzz

Who founded Crewzz?

Oren Dagan founded Crewzz after two decades of construction and remodeling experience revealed the need for one homeowner-centered structure around residential design, construction, decisions, documentation, and long-term care.

What is Oren Dagan's construction background?

Oren founded SOD Home Group in 2008. His experience inside residential construction and remodeling informed the development of Crewzz and its Stewardship model.

How are Crewzz and SOD Home Group related?

Oren founded both companies. SOD Home Group is a separate design-build firm. Its history demonstrates Oren's experience, but it is not presented as Crewzz's project record.

Why did Oren create Crewzz?

He saw that capable professionals could still operate inside a fragmented structure that left the homeowner responsible for connecting the whole. Crewzz was created to provide one accountable Stewardship framework around that experience.

What does Stewardship mean at Crewzz?

Stewardship is Crewzz's homeowner-centered framework for coordinating the agreed decisions, information, responsibilities, documentation, and handoffs across a residential project. Appropriate licensed professionals remain responsible for work within their disciplines.
