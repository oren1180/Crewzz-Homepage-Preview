The integration process

Designed for the body that lives there.

A wellness room is easy to add. A residence that quietly supports the household from morning to night is harder. Crewzz and MAVI bring longevity considerations into the project early enough to coordinate with architecture, interiors, engineering, equipment, controls, construction, and the way you intend to live.

Step 01

Begin with the residence, the household, and the project.

We review the property, project stage, location, priorities, confidentiality needs, and the specialist team required to determine whether the Estate offering is an appropriate fit.

Concept imagery

This is not a shopping list of wellness products. It is a coordinated way to consider the residence as one environmental system, then carry approved recommendations to the people responsible for designing, engineering, building, testing, and operating it.

The goal is simple: fewer disconnected decisions, less wellness theater, and a home whose invisible systems receive the same attention as its visible finishes. Exact services, targets, testing, responsibilities, and specialist involvement are defined for each accepted project.

Ten interconnected domains

Your body notices what the eye cannot see.

The air at the pillow. The water at every fixture. The light your eyes receive after sunset. The sound that follows you into the bedroom. The materials surrounding your family. These conditions are easy to overlook because they are part of the background. Your body still experiences them.

MAVI’s framework considers air, water, light, sound, temperature, materials, microbiology, technology, mind, and habits as connected conditions within the residence. Research can inform the specification, but it does not turn a home into medical treatment or guarantee a health outcome.

Straight answers

Before you apply.

What is longevity architecture?

Longevity architecture applies research-informed environmental considerations to the design and operation of a home. It can include air, water, light, sound, temperature, materials, technology, and the daily patterns the residence supports.

Who can apply?

Available to Crewzz Estate clients only. Projects are reviewed for fit, geography, timing, specialist availability, and current capacity.

Is this medical care?

No. This is residential planning, coordination, and educational information. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a guarantee of sleep, recovery, longevity, or any other health outcome.

Can this be introduced into an existing residence?

Potentially. Feasibility depends on the home, existing systems, project scope, specialist input, budget, and the extent of renovation being considered.
