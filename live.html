How it works

Maintain the home with the same intention used to create it.

LIVE begins by organizing what is known about the residence. The record, care plan, providers, service history, and future decisions stay connected to the home instead of disappearing across inboxes, invoices, and individual vendors.

Step 01

Give the house a memory.

Plans, systems information, warranties, service history, contacts, and known conditions are organized into one property record.

Concept imagery

Every home is different. Reviews, schedules, service categories, providers, response expectations, records, and responsibilities are defined in the service agreement.

Crewzz coordinates the program. Licensed, regulated, and specialist work remains with appropriately qualified providers. Your property record belongs to you, whether you remain enrolled or not.

The reason why

The most expensive maintenance problem is often the one nobody was watching.

Homes rarely fail all at once. They drift. A seal opens. A drain slows. A filter is missed. A small stain becomes familiar enough to ignore. Different providers see individual symptoms, while the owner is left to connect the pattern.

Coordinated home care cannot promise that nothing will go wrong. It creates a repeatable rhythm for attention, documentation, and response before every issue becomes an emergency.

Earlier attentionProperty recordsFewer loose endsQualified providersInformed decisionsProperty continuity

What continuity protects

A home should not have to start over every time someone new arrives.

For months or years, people know what sits inside the walls, which trade installed each system, and why decisions were made. Then the project closes and that knowledge walks away. LIVE gives the residence a record that stays with the property. For an existing home, the record begins with the documents and conditions that can be established at enrollment.

  1. 01

    The record

    Plans, warranties, systems information, prior service, and known conditions organized around the property.

  2. 02

    The rhythm

    Recurring care and review organized into a plan for the residence and its systems.

  3. 03

    The response

    Issues begin with property history before the next provider starts diagnosing from zero.

  4. 04

    The providers

    Qualified trades and specialists coordinated for the work defined in the service agreement.

  5. 05

    The history

    Service, observations, decisions, and changes added to the property record over time.

  6. 06

    The perspective

    A periodic view across the home, not only the individual issue demanding attention today.

Straight answers

Before you join the waitlist.

What is Crewzz LIVE?

Crewzz LIVE is a coordinated home care relationship for completed residences. It organizes property information, planned care, provider coordination, issue response, and ongoing records under a property-specific agreement.

Does Crewzz perform every maintenance service?

No. Crewzz coordinates the home care program. Licensed, regulated, and specialist work is performed by appropriately qualified providers under the service agreement and local requirements.

Is enrollment open?

No. Current enrollment is full. Homeowners may join the waitlist to be considered when suitable capacity becomes available.

Can I join the waitlist if Crewzz did not build my home?

Yes. You may submit a waitlist inquiry. Acceptance depends on the residence, documentation, location, service requirements, operational fit, and future capacity.

Who owns the property record?

You do. The property record belongs to the homeowner and remains yours whether you stay enrolled or not. This policy must also appear in the final service agreement.

Does LIVE guarantee that problems will be prevented?

No. Every residence changes over time, and no maintenance program eliminates every failure or emergency. LIVE creates an organized structure for attention, records, coordination, and decisions within the service agreement.

What happens after I join?

Your information enters the LIVE waitlist. If capacity becomes available for a residence like yours, Crewzz may contact you to determine whether the program is a fit.
